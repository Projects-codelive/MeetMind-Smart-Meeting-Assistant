## Animation Principles

### Timing
- **Standard:** 300ms
- **Entrance:** 500-600ms
- **Counters:** 2000ms
- **Progress bars:** 1500ms
- **Stagger:** 100ms between items

### Easing
- **Entrance:** `[0.23, 1, 0.32, 1]` (cubic-bezier)
- **Hover:** `easeOut`
- **Spring:** `stiffness: 300, damping: 20`
- **Linear:** For continuous animations
